MYIO

Client
Venture
Duration
6 Weeks
Role
Product Designer
Tools
Figma · Illustrator · Webflow
Project Description:

Full UX/UI case study for My 10 — a top-10 list maker app. The project covers number psychology research, platform fragmentation analysis, affinity mapping, and a complete design pipeline from branding through high-fidelity screens, user flow, and design refinement testing.

Key Deliverables:
  • UX Research & Affinity Mapping
  • Brand Identity & Design System
  • Hand drawn & Digital Wireframes
  • User Flow Diagram
  • Fully Compiled PDF
  • App Visualiser

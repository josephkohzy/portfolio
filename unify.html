Project Description:

A workplace communication platform that eliminates cross-platform friction by consolidating chat, email, reminders, and team coordination into a single, cohesive experience. This UI/UX case study covers the full design process: from user research and persona development through a final User experience Prototype -> for a product built to fix workplace communication.

Key Deliverables:
  • UX Research
  • Branding Charts
  • UI web and app designs
  • Mograph visualiser

The Core Problem

Communication is
broken at work

88%
of the workweek is now spent communicating across fragmented channels
84%
of business leaders are communicating in more channels than ever before
$9.3k
lost per worker annually due to poor communication through multiple channels
Problem

Fragmentation kills focus

Slack. Teams. Gmail. Notion. Zoom. Users switch contexts 4-6x per hour: critical decisions get buried, priorities disappear between apps.

Solution

One ecosystem, zero friction

Chat, email, reminders, and org in one shared workspace. Forward an email into a chat thread. Create a reminder from a message. Less switching. One Direct Channel.

User Research : Personas

Who Unify is
designed for

These are three featured personas built from common profiles across team leads, managers, and workers that communicate in corporate. [Management and platform issues are not mutually exclusive]

Sarah Tan
Project Manager

Manages 4 cross-functional teams. Constantly juggling Slack, email, and Asana. Her day is reactive, borderline chaotic.

▲Switching 3+ messaging tools daily
▲Some decisions missed in email threads
▲Reminders split across 3 apps
▲No single source of truth for follow-ups
Jason Lim
Startup Founder

Runs a 12 person startup where time is a scarcity. Needs alignment without office tooling overhead. Every minute counts.

▲Too many platforms for a lean budget
▲Critical talk split across Slack & Gmail
▲External emails get lost, follow-ups missed
▲scattered attention across teams
Priya Sharma
Remote Employee

Senior designer across 3 time zones. Async-first but constantly misses contextually important updates scattered across tools.

▲Notifications scattered across 4 apps
▲Action items from emails buried & forgotten
▲No thread linking chat to source email
▲Missed assets requires revisiting read emails

Feature Architecture

Everything your team
needs in one place

4 deeply interconnected core modules:

Unified Messaging

Real-time chat with full threading, reactions, read receipts, and intelligent notification grouping.

🧵
Threaded Conversations
Inline reply threads inside any chat or group
🔔
Smart Notifications
Grouped, prioritized, silenceable alerts
📌
Pinned Messages
Bookmark critical messages for team reference
🔍
Unified Search
Search messages, emails and reminders in one query

Integrated Email

A full email client inside Unify: Connected to your work identity, bridged directly into team chats.

📬
Unified Inbox
All accounts in one view with labels and filters
↗
Email-to-Chat Forward
Forward emails into chat threads with context preserved
🎭
Sender Aliases
Send as your department, role, or personal identity
↩
Inline Replies
Reply to emails without leaving your current conversation

Smart Reminders

Reminders that live inside your conversations: Created from messages, shared with teammates, threaded for discussion.

⏰
Personal Reminders
Time and event-based triggers tied to your workflow
📤
Shared Team Reminders
Assign reminders to coworkers or broadcast to a team
💬
Reminder Threads
Attach a discussion thread to any reminder for context
⚡
Create from Message
Long-press any chat or email to set a follow-up reminder

People & Organisation

A live company directory that respects hierarchy, surfaces the right people, and stays context-aware.

📋
Company Directory
Browse colleagues by name, role, department, or location
🗂
Teams & Departments
Structured org groups with shared channels and reminders
🔒
Role-Based Visibility
Admins control what each role can see and administer
⚡
Quick Contact Actions
Message, email, or set a reminder from any profile in one tap

Ideation - Roadmap

What ships first,
what comes next

A prioritized split between the MVP core and extended vision.

MVP: Core Release

Ship First
💬Direct messaging and group chats with threading
✉Integrated email client with inbox management
🔁Email to chat forwarding
🔔Personal and shared reminders
📋Company directory and basic team groupings

Extended Vision

Next Phases

🧵Reminder threads with full discussion capability
🗓Calendar integration and reminder triggers
🔌Third-party integrations: Notion, Linear, GitHub, Jira
📱Desktop client with persistent sidebar
